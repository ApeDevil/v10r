# Supplier report Q3 2026

## Summary
Delivery performance of our main outdoor supplier Bergwerk dropped sharply in the third quarter.
All other suppliers stayed within their agreed service levels.

## Bergwerk
Bergwerk moved its backpack and sleeping-gear production to a new plant in Brno at the beginning
of July. Since the move, lead times have grown and more orders arrive late. The account manager
expects the new plant to reach full capacity "in the first half of next year" but has not
committed to a date.

Bergwerk supplies our two largest product lines, backpacks and sleeping gear. Late deliveries in
August and September led to empty shelves for the Urban and Trail backpack series in several
stores.

## Other suppliers
- Alpenglas (kitchen and accessories): on time, no complaints.
- Lumen&Co (lighting): on time; price increase of 4% announced for January.
- Polarfux (sleeping bags, small volume): on time.

## Options discussed
- Keep Bergwerk and agree on a recovery plan with monthly reviews.
- Qualify a second supplier for backpacks; a qualification usually takes four to six months.
- Increase safety stock for the Urban series until delivery is stable again.

## Next steps
Purchasing to decide in the October meeting whether Bergwerk goes on the watch list.
