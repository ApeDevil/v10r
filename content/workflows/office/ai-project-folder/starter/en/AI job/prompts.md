# Prompts for the six rounds

Copy one prompt at a time into your AI tool. Change the date and the file names.

## 1. Brief
Read README.md. Then look at the files in input/ and tell me, in your own words, what they
contain and what you are going to do. Don't change anything yet.

## 2. Correct
I updated README.md. Read it again and tell me what changed in your plan.

## 3. Plan
Write the plan as a numbered list, and wait for my OK.

## 4. Do
Go ahead. Put the script in tools/ and the result in output/<date>/.

## 5. Check
Check the result against "Done when" in README.md and list anything that fails.

## 6. Record
Add the decisions we made today to the Decisions section of README.md.

## Next time
New files in input/. Run the script in tools/ and tell me what is different from last time.
